**HTWE OO MYANMAR** is a premier traditional Burmese puppet theatre ensemble, co-founded by master puppeteer **Khin Maung Htwe** and his wife **Tin Oo**. The name "Htwe Oo" meaningfully combines their surnames to symbolize a shared passion for preserving Myanmar's rich cultural heritage.

### 🎭 Preserving 800 Years of Cultural Legacy

Established to safeguard Myanmar’s **over 800-year-old traditional string puppetry (Yoke Thé)**, Htwe Oo Myanmar stands dedicated to authentic performance arts. While many contemporary shows shifted toward simplified commercial tours, our ensemble collaborates closely with veteran master puppeteers to revive, protect, and pass down ancient marionette craftsmanship and classical stage technique.

### 🌐 Global Reach & Cultural Milestones

HTWE OO MYANMAR performs regularly across Myanmar and actively brings traditional marionette art to audiences worldwide:

**First-Ever Traditional Puppet Show in Laos:** Successfully presented a historic performance at Hotel Lexi, Vientiane (10 October 2026).

**Myanmar Puppet & Jazz/POP Fusion:** Featured at Vintage Pratunam in Bangkok (30 August 2026).

**International & Cultural Showcases:** Hosting performances in Yangon and international cultural stages, welcoming local families and global visitors.

**European Tour & 50th International Milestone:** Honored to reach our **50th International Participation** representing Myanmar's intangible heritage across European cultural platforms, including upcoming showcases in Italy.
