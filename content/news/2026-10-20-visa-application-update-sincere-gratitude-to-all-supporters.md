# #StepByStepToMilan 🎭✨

Yesterday was quite a rush! Between running errands and getting caught in traffic, I couldn't update everyone right away. 

Good news arrived from the VFS office: **"No worries! If your passport is held by us, we will process and issue it for you."** 

Checking the schedule, if things go smoothly, we might be able to fly out by the 15th or latest by the 22nd. Because of this, we stopped by the Bangkok VFS office to double-check and inquire about our visa processing. 

After checking the dates and timelines, we felt much relieved and just went with the flow! As for the courier tracking service, we are tracking it closely—it should arrive just in time before our departure. 

A heartfelt thank you to everyone supporting our **#GoFundMe** campaign. And to those who hand-delivered contributions directly to us—we are deeply grateful for your kindness. 

Some generous friends are also helping us out with bank transfers, and we’ve received heartfelt support in Myanmar Kyats via KBZ Pay and AYA Pay as well. 

Thank you all so much from the bottom of our hearts!
