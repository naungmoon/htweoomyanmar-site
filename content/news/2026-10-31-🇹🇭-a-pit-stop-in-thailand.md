Can’t we fly straight from Phnom Penh to Milan?

Of course we can! Last year, we flew Cathay Pacific via Hong Kong (Phnom Penh ➔ Hong Kong ➔ Milan). 

Sure, we could also transit through Singapore, but well... Singapore Airlines tickets don’t come cheap! 😉

That’s exactly why we chose Bangkok—a major transit hub offering plenty of budget-friendly flight options. 

How much did we actually save? Well, enough that the price of **one ticket** on other routes covered **both of us** flying via Bangkok!

Even within Bangkok, airfares vary widely depending on the airline—whether it’s Thai Airways, Emirates, Qatar Airways, and so on. Thai Airways offers a direct Bangkok-to-Milan flight, making it the most premium and expensive option. 

Airlines with a layover in the Middle East tend to be quite a bit more affordable, with prices depending on how long you have to wait at the transit airport.

So, which airline will Htwe & Oo be taking next? And where will our next layover be? Stay tuned to follow our travel journey! ❤️
