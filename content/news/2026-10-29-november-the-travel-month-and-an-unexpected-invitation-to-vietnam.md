# November: The Travel Month and Unexpected Good News ✈️🎭

Aunty Oo casually said to me the other day, *"My feet are feeling so itchy, I think I'm bound to travel!"* 

I chuckled and replied, *"Well, of course! We're heading to Italy soon, remember?"* 
She laughed back and said, *"Nope, that's not it. I have a feeling there's more!"* And who am I to argue with her intuition? 😆

Lo and behold, out of nowhere, another wonderful opportunity landed right in our laps! We just received an official invitation from an International Puppet Festival hosted on the beautiful **Phu Quoc Island in Vietnam**!

At first, there was a tiny back-and-forth about whether ticket coverage was included or not. But since Phu Quoc is practically right next door to Phnom Penh, we gave a big thumbs up ✅ without hesitation! The festival will run for 10 full days, from the last week of November into the first week of December.

Representing Myanmar's traditional cultural heritage as a humble grain of sand on international stages is always our greatest pride. We’ll do our absolute best in every way we can!

The best part? No travel fundraising is needed for this Vietnam trip at all—just bring your warm smiles and encouragement!

Now, don't think every post of mine ends up at the same station... but **only 4 days remain** for our Milan Award Ceremony journey! We'd deeply appreciate any last-mile support to help us reach the finish line.
