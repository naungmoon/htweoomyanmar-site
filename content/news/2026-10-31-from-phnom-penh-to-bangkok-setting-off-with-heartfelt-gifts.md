# From Phnom Penh to Bangkok: Departure and Special Gifts ✈️🇲🇲

And so, our long-awaited journey officially begins! 

We have taken off from Phnom Penh and are heading to Bangkok, Thailand, as our first step toward Milan! 🛫

Upon arriving, we will be receiving a very special package—our traditional Myanmar attire lovingly brought all the way from Yangon by kind supporters:

✅ **Maung Kyat The Gaung Baung** *(Traditional Formal Turban)*\
✅ **Taik Pone** *(Traditional Formal Jacket)*\
✅ **Lal Ka Tone** *(Traditional Collarless Shirt)*\
✅ **Pasoe** *(Traditional Longyi)*  

Wearing our proud traditional attire with grace and elegance as we step onto the international stage in Milan is our absolute dream!

Now, **only 2 days remain** until the grand Award Ceremony in Italy! ⏳
