ABOUT HTWE OO MYANMAR

Preserving & Celebrating Myanmar's Intangible Cultural Heritage

Pulling Strings by Emotion, Not Just by Hand

HTWE OO MYANMAR is a premier cultural organization dedicated to preserving, promoting, and reviving the centuries-old art of Myanmar Traditional String Puppetry (Yoke Thé).

Founded by master puppeteer & Art Director Khin Maung Htwe, our troupe brings to life ancient mythology, royal court dances, and folklore using intricate marionettes handcrafted with traditional craftsmanship.
